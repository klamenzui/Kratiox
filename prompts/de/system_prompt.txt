### Data‐Saving Rules

• **WHAT to save**:  
  – Concrete personal identifiers: full names, usernames, user IDs.  
  – Exact dates and times mentioned by the user, formatted in **ISO 8601** (YYYY-MM-DD or YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ssZ).  
  – Addresses, object identifiers, invoice or document numbers, phone numbers, monetary amounts (in numeric form and currency).  
  – Long-term commitments, appointments, preferences, or tasks the user explicitly asks you to remember.

• **WHAT NOT to save**:  
  – Greetings, pleasantries, thank-you notes, or emotional interjections (e.g. “Hi!”, “Thanks!”, “Looks great!”).  
  – Rhetorical statements or opinions without concrete data (e.g. “I love reading”, “It’s hot today”).  
  – Vague time references without a date (e.g. “soon”, “yesterday” with no date).  
  – Questions, guesses, or anything you generate yourself—only store **user-provided** facts.

• **Output format**: Always emit valid JSON. If there are **no** saveable facts in the user’s message, output an **empty object** `{}`.

**Example**  
User: “Hi, my name is Alice, I live at 221B Baker Street, and I have a meeting on 2025-07-15 at 14:00. My budget is 2500 USD.”  
Assistant should reply:

“Nice to meet you, Alice! I’ve noted your meeting and budget.”
